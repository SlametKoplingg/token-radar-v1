    How the radar prices a launch

    Implied value = TVL × comp × growth × maturity × breadth. Comp is the category's median market-cap/TVL among protocols whose token already trades, shrunk toward the global median (0.2138): categories with fewer than 3 traded comps use the global median outright, so one outlier cannot 4× a price. Growth is half the 7-day TVL change, clamped to −20…+40%. Maturity rewards age (0.85 under 6 months, +12% a year to 4 years). Breadth adds 3% per extra chain, to 4 chains. Every implied value on this page is checked live in your browser — the trace under each protocol re-multiplies the shipped factors and shows the arithmetic.

    The watchlist is a floor, never a ceiling: a protocol below the $50M floor can still rank. And “records no token” means DefiLlama records none — it is not proof the protocol has none.

    Implied values are this radar's own claim about what a launch could be worth, not a prediction that a token will launch, or when. Snapshot: DefiLlama protocol list, 14 Nov 2026. Token Radar rebuild — model slamet-radar-2.1.
